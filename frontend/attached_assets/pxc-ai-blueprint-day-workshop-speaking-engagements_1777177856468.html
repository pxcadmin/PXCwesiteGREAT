Four Topic Areas

Workshop Topics

Jennifer speaks and trains on all four of these topic areas. Each can be delivered as a standalone session or combined into a full-day intensive.

Topic 01 — AI in Healthcare
🤖

AI Integration in Healthcare — Safe, Effective, and Human-Led

For: Practice owners, administrators, clinical leaders, medical associations

Participants leave with:
Their practice's AI Readiness Score™ (personalized, during the session)
Understanding of what AI can and cannot do safely in a clinical environment
A customized AI Integration Opportunity Map (10–15 prioritized use cases)
Medical Practice AI Governance Framework overview
90-Day AI Implementation Roadmap
Key topics covered:
Ambient documentation AI Call triage automation Patient communication No-show recovery Scheduling optimization HIPAA compliance for AI Vendor evaluation Self-Funding AI ROI
Who should attend

Any practice leadership team considering AI adoption — especially those who have been burned by tech promises before.

Topic 02 — Leadership Development
🧭

Leadership Development for Practice Leaders

For: Physician-owners, medical directors, practice administrators, office managers

Participants leave with:
Personal leadership style assessment
The three accountability frameworks that replace micromanagement
Decision-making frameworks that eliminate leadership bottlenecks
A 30-day leadership behavior change plan
Tools for having the hard conversations — staff performance, culture expectations, physician alignment
Key topics covered:
Leadership strain cycle Emotional intelligence in healthcare Self-running practice model Managing vs. leading AI & leadership bandwidth
Who should attend

Practice leaders who are stuck in firefighter mode and want to actually lead.

Topic 03 — Culture Transformation
🌱

Culture Transformation in Medical Practices

For: Practice administrators, HR leaders, physician-owners, department heads

Participants leave with:
Culture diagnosis for their team (what's working, what's broken, and why)
The staff engagement frameworks that reduce turnover
Recognition systems that cost nothing and change everything
The communication protocols that align staff and providers
Implementation plan for the first 30 days
Key topics covered:
Culture vs. people problems Culture & patient experience AI Culture Gap (46% stat) Building buy-in for change AI Champions model
Who should attend

Any organization experiencing high turnover, staff disengagement, or inconsistent performance — the symptoms of a culture that isn't supporting its people.

Topic 04 — Patient Experience
❤️

Patient Experience Excellence — What Actually Moves the Needle

For: Practice owners, administrators, patient experience leads, clinical leaders

Participants leave with:
Patient experience touchpoint audit of their practice
The three trust-building moments every visit contains
Patient communication redesign (phone, text, email templates)
The consent conversation framework for AI tools in the exam room
An online reputation management strategy they can implement this week
Key topics covered:
Clinical excellence & reviews Experience Instability Trap™ Patient vs. provider perception Jennifer's patient perspective Unified PXC System™
Who should attend

Any practice that wants to improve patient satisfaction scores, online reviews, and patient retention — especially those where scores are "mysteriously" inconsistent.
